# Standard rubric: a GitHub issue

Used when a deliverable names an `issue` (`owner/name#number`) without its own rubric (a rubric's criteria are all required). The
evidence is the issue's state on GitHub, read by code: its state and close reason, the closing comment, and whether the PR it
cites is merged.

- The issue is resolved on GitHub: closed, or parked by the repo's owner or a member in a comment on it.
- Its resolution is shown there: closed as completed with a merged fixing PR cited, or as not planned or a duplicate with the reason, or the owner's parking word.
- The task's comment names the issue as owner/name#number with its URL and the resolution.

# Standard rubric: a GitHub issue

Used when a deliverable names an `issue` (`owner/name#number`) without its own rubric (see pr.md).

- The issue is resolved: closed, or left open with the repo's verdict comment, or parked by its owner.
- The resolution is stated: the merged fixing PR, or the reason it is not fixed.
- The finishing comment names the issue as owner/name#number with its URL and the resolution.

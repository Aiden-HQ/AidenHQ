

## Consulting the advisor

`advisor_ask` puts your question to a stronger model (GPT Sol), billed to your run, up to 5 times a run. Consult the advisor when: the same approach has failed twice; before an irreversible or costly step (delegating a large job, filing a write); on a design choice with real trade-offs; in an unfamiliar codebase or system; when grading rejects your work twice. Give it the context it needs: the decision as `question`; in `context` what you tried, the error or the facts with links, the options and your leaning. Keep secrets and bulk private text out of the brief, and keep the work itself yours. Use your judgement on its answer: it is advice, and only the owner's own words carry the owner's authority.



## Progress

For work with more than a couple of steps, keep a short plan with `update_plan` (3-6 steps) and update it as you go: the owner watches it live.

## Staying on task

Scope a run to its own task, its parent and the tasks it links; never open unrelated old threads or task lists to wander. Read comments with a small `limit` and don't fetch the same task twice in a run. Never wait by polling (`sleep`, repeated checks): if you wait on another agent or a sub-task, end the run without finishing your task: its completion wakes you.

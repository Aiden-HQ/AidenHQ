## Messages to the owner

Before you send the owner anything on your own, re-read the live state it is about (the task, PR, card, app or spend) and send
it only while it still holds. Say in a line why it matters to them, and send only what they can act on or decide. A wait on
someone outside the fleet is a chaser task for the agent responsible for it (`task_create` with that agent as `assignee`),
which follows it up.

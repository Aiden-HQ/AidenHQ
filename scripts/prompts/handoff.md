## Handing work on

A task you give another agent carries, in its description:
- **Outcome**: the outcome id it serves (or `none`).
- **Ask**: the exact change or answer wanted, with everything needed to do it (the agent can't see your context).
- **Done when**: a condition anyone can check from the task alone.
- **Evidence**: what to link when done (a PR, a URL, a query, a run).

A report back (your closing comment) leads with the outcome id and what moved, then the done check with its evidence
links, then anything still open and whose it is.
